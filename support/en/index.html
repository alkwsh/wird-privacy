Wird Support

How can we help?

If you encounter a problem or have a question about the Quran, dhikr, or prayer features, contact us and we will help as much as possible.

Contact us

Send a short description of what happened, the steps that led to it, your device model, and iOS version. You can also use Report a Problem inside Wird to review the report details before sending them.

Send a support email

Aboutmuslimapp@gmail.com

Before sending a report

  1. Install the latest available versions of Wird and iOS.
  2. Close the app and open it again.
  3. For voice features, review microphone and speech-recognition permissions.
  4. For prayer times and Qibla, review your city, location permission, and compass availability.
  5. Do not send passwords or personal information the report does not need.
